▸ Advanced / Beta Features ⓘ

Experimental tools. Behavior may change between releases.

Overhang Protection

Some textures push surface vertices downward, creating new overhangs. When enabled, vertices are never moved in the −Z direction during texturing. X and Y movement is unaffected.

Smooth Bottom

Snap any vertex within 0.1 mm of the bottom plane onto it after texturing. Keeps the bed-contact surface perfectly flat so slicers don't shade tiny height variations on bottom slivers.

Harvest Flat Faces

When decimation reaches the triangle limit there are often still flat faces left that cost almost nothing to remove. With this on, decimation keeps collapsing them past the limit — as long as each collapse moves the surface less than the tolerance below — saving extra triangles without losing visible detail.

Preserve Untextured Surfaces

Keep surfaces that receive no texture (painted mask, angle masks) exactly as they are in the source model: mesh regularization and decimation will not move or remove any of their vertices, so fillets and fine details stay intact. If the untextured surfaces alone exceed the triangle limit, the export exceeds the limit instead of degrading the texture.

Bake Textures

Apply the current texture to the mesh and reload the result as the working model so you can keep editing.

Welcome to BrailleMesh

      
Add Braille signage and displacement textures to your 3D prints — directly in your browser, no cloud and completely free. A fork of BumpMesh by CNC Kitchen: the whole texture toolset stays, and a full Braille workspace comes on top, open by default.


      
Quick start

      
    
        
  1. Import an STL, OBJ, 3MF or STEP.
    2. 
        
  2. Type a word and click Add — it becomes a Braille block.
    3. 
        
  3. Drag the block onto a face of the model.
    4. 
        
  4. Fine-tune it — raised or embedded, dot size, readable caption — and export as STL or 3MF.
    5. 
      

      
For displacement textures, switch to the Texture workspace in the header.


      
What BrailleMesh adds — October 2026

      
    
        
  • The Braille workspace (open by default): type a word, add it as a block, drag it onto any face of the model. Eight Braille systems: German Vollschrift and Kurzschrift, English UEB Grade 1 and 2, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese. Every placement keeps its own position, rotation and name; clicking it swings the view there.
    • 
        
  • Raised or embedded: the dots stand proud of the surface, or the whole block sits in one recessed frame with the dot tops flush with the surface. Every block carries its own parameters: dot diameter, dot/cell/word/row spacing, dot height and rounding, alignment, raster resolution.
    • 
        
  • Readable caption: on request, the word is also written as black print under each Braille line — raised or engraved — so sighted readers can check it.
    • 
        
  • Separate-part export: in the exported 3MF the Braille is its own printable volume beside the base body, so you can colour it independently in the slicer.
    • 
        
  • No default texture: the example cube starts plain; the map you had active in your last session comes back on its own.
    • 
        
  • From the base project (BumpMesh v1.4.3 by CNC Kitchen): 101 displacement textures in eight categories, texture layers, section view, STEP import, SpaceMouse support, 16 languages. Base project changelog.
